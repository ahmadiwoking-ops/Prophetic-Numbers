What's most useful to send

A correction. If a count is wrong, a reaction is misstated, or a citation doesn't say what it's claimed to say, please point to the specific line. Errors get fixed and acknowledged.

A rival reading. If the sequence resolves better under some other framework, that is worth far more than another argument for this one.

A statistical test. The open question nobody has answered is how often a random sequence of this length and alphabet would throw up comparable patterns. If you have the background to run that properly, please do get in touch.

Manuscript detail. Anything bearing on the original document — the hand-drawn symbol, the row breaks, variant printings — would help.

Your address is used only to reply. Nothing is stored on this site and nothing is passed on.
