Official Websites

Where to read and watch more

These are the community's own official platforms. Each opens in a new tab.

Al Islam ↗ alislam.org

The official website of the Ahmadiyya Muslim Community — books, articles, the Holy Qur'an in many languages, and multimedia.

 MTA International ↗ mta.tv

The community's 24-hour satellite television channel, broadcasting worldwide in many languages, free to watch online.

 Review of Religions ↗ reviewofreligions.org

A comparative-religion magazine founded in 1902 by the Promised Messiah — and the publication that issued the 2022 codebreaker appeal.

 Al Hakam ↗ alhakam.org

A weekly English newspaper for members of the Ahmadiyya Muslim Jamaat around the world.

 True Islam ↗ trueislam.co.uk

An introduction to the beliefs and teachings of Islam as understood by the Ahmadiyya Muslim Community. (Check this URL and edit if you prefer a different True Islam site.)

 Khalifa of Islam ↗ khalifaofislam.com

About His Holiness Mirza Masroor Ahmad, fifth Khalifa of the Ahmadiyya Muslim Community — his sermons, speeches and work for peace.
